# Strategy Engine Heuristics v1

## Core Question

Given the facts, documents, posture, authority, contradictions, and credibility findings:

What should counsel do next?

## Inputs

* procedural posture
* claims
* defenses
* evidence
* contradictions
* credibility findings
* attack surface findings
* authority

## Strategy Categories

### Offensive

Examples:

* summary judgment
* dismissal
* sanctions
* preclusion
* impeachment

### Defensive

Examples:

* cure pleading defect
* amend pleading
* supplement evidence
* narrow claims
* preserve arguments

### Discovery

Examples:

* subpoena
* deposition
* document demand
* third-party records
* expert discovery

### Settlement

Examples:

* leverage event
* weak damages
* credibility concerns
* litigation cost pressure

## Output

The engine should answer:

1. What matters most?
2. What is strongest?
3. What is weakest?
4. What should happen next?
5. What evidence is missing?
6. What motion is most viable?
