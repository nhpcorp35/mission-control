# Litigation Workflow — Attorney Heuristics v1

## Core Insight

Legal AI should not behave like a generic legal search engine.

The attorney workflow is:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Identify the court and department.
3. Find similar cases.
4. Pull the full docket.
5. Review the filings.
6. Extract the roadmap.
7. Adapt the roadmap.
8. Draft.
9. Verify authority.

## What Lawyers Actually Want

Not just:

* cases
* citations

Also:

* complaints
* answers
* motions
* oppositions
* replies
* memoranda
* exhibits
* orders

The filings are often more valuable than the published decision.
