# Attorney Heuristics v1

## Law Is Not Deterministic

Law is not a simple rules engine.

Law is:

* probabilistic
* fact dependent
* judge dependent
* procedure dependent

## Judges Hate

* lies
* contradictions
* perjury
* discovery abuse
* procedural gamesmanship
* reversal risk

## Litigation Is Pattern Matching

Lawyers frequently:

* find similar cases
* review filings
* reuse structures
* adapt successful arguments

## Authority Hierarchy Matters

Always determine:

* jurisdiction
* department
* court level

before evaluating authority.

## Strong Lawyers

Strong lawyers do not merely cite cases.

They:

* connect facts to elements
* connect elements to authority
* connect authority to relief requested

## Contradictions Matter

A contradiction is often more valuable than ten supporting cases.

Examples:

* deposition vs affidavit
* complaint vs evidence
* motion vs testimony
* witness vs document

## Credibility Matters

Credibility attacks frequently determine outcomes.

Questions:

* Who changed their story?
* Who lacks support?
* Who is contradicted by documents?
* Who benefits from the contradiction?

## Search Is Not The Goal

Search is a tool.

The goal is:

* understanding
* strategy
* persuasion
* outcome

## Product Direction

Legal AI should prioritize:

1. Issue detection
2. Contradiction detection
3. Credibility analysis
4. Attack surface analysis
5. Strategy generation

before generic drafting.
