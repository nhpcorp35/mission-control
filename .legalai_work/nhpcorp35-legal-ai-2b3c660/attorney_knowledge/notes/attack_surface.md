# Attack Surface Heuristics v1

## Core Principle

Every case has vulnerable points.

The goal is to identify where an opponent is exposed.

## Categories

### Pleading Defects

Examples:

* missing element
* conclusory allegations
* inconsistent allegations
* jurisdictional defects
* standing defects

### Evidence Defects

Examples:

* missing documents
* unsupported allegations
* authentication problems
* hearsay dependence

### Credibility Defects

Examples:

* inconsistent statements
* timeline problems
* conflicting testimony
* unexplained changes

### Procedural Defects

Examples:

* service defects
* motion defects
* verification defects
* filing defects
* preservation issues

### Damages Defects

Examples:

* unsupported damages
* speculative damages
* causation gaps

### Authority Defects

Examples:

* outdated authority
* non-controlling authority
* distinguishable authority
* missing authority

## Ranking Factors

Attack points should be ranked by:

* likelihood of success
* impact on claims
* impact on defenses
* credibility effect
* procedural leverage

## Engine Output

The engine should identify:

* weakness
* supporting evidence
* affected claim
* litigation value
* recommended use

## Goal

The system should answer:

"Where is the opponent weakest?"
